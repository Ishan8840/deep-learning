# 02 · Generative models

These topics move from compact representations to iterative generation:

1. [Variational autoencoder](01-vae/README.md) learns a distribution over latent representations and decodes samples from it.
2. [Denoising diffusion](02-ddpm/README.md) learns to reverse a noising process one step at a time.

The [robot policies](../04-robot-policies/README.md) chapter then uses related generative ideas to produce **action sequences** instead of images, including [flow matching](../04-robot-policies/03-flow-matching-policy/README.md).
